Pricing & savings

Is the statement analysis really free?

Yes — no cost, no obligation, no strings. Send one recent processing statement and you get a written breakdown of your effective rate and every fee on the account. If your current pricing is already competitive, we will say so and you keep the analysis.

What is an "effective rate" and why does it matter?

Your effective rate is total processing fees divided by total card volume for the month. It folds every percentage, per-item charge and monthly fee into one number. Quoted rates hide behind tiers and qualifications; the effective rate cannot hide anything, which makes it the only fair way to compare processors.

How much can I expect to save?

It depends on your current pricing model, card mix, average ticket and monthly volume. Businesses on tiered or flat-rate pricing typically have the most room; accounts already on tight interchange-plus pricing may have very little. Rather than guess, we show you the actual math from your own statement.

What is the difference between tiered, flat-rate and interchange-plus pricing?
  • Tiered: transactions are sorted into qualified, mid-qualified and non-qualified buckets. The processor decides what lands where, so costs are unpredictable.
  • Flat-rate: one simple percentage for everything. Easy to read, but debit and low-cost cards subsidize the processor's margin.
  • Interchange-plus: you pay the true card-network cost plus a disclosed markup. It is the most transparent structure and usually the least expensive for established volume.
Are there setup or application fees?

No application fee and no charge for the analysis. If hardware is needed, we quote it up front in writing so you can compare purchase versus other options — and we will always review an existing equipment lease before you commit to anything new.

Programs & contracts

What is a zero-fee or cash discount program?

It is a compliant dual-pricing structure: customers paying cash get a discounted price, while the card price includes the cost of acceptance. With correct signage, receipt language and terminal programming, it can bring your net processing cost close to zero. We handle the compliance side and give your staff a simple way to explain it.

Will a cash discount program upset my customers?

In most businesses the reaction is mild, especially when pricing is posted clearly and staff can explain it in one sentence. It is not right for everyone though — some customer bases and some industries do better on traditional low-rate pricing. During your review we will tell you honestly which direction we would recommend.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Accounts are placed month-to-month with no early termination fee. If the pricing or the service ever stops making sense, you can leave.

I am still under contract with my current processor. Can you help?

Often, yes. We will review your existing agreement, identify the actual termination terms, and lay out what leaving would cost versus what staying is costing. Sometimes the right advice is to wait a few months — and we will say that if it is true.

Are you a bank or a processor?

Neither. We are an independent merchant services consultancy. We analyze your statement, recommend the right structure, place the account with an established processor and bank, and remain your point of contact for service afterward.

Setup, equipment & funding

How long does setup take?

Most applications are approved in one to two business days. Equipment ships right after approval, so a straightforward switch is usually live within about a week. We stay on the phone with you through your first batch and first deposit.

Can I keep my current POS system?

Frequently, yes. Many popular POS platforms can be re-pointed to new processing without replacing hardware. Some systems are locked to a single processor — in that case we price a replacement, build your menu or item catalog, and program it before it ships.

How fast will I get my money?

Standard funding is typically one to two business days after batch settlement, with next-day funding available on eligible accounts. The biggest controllable factor is batching before the daily cutoff — we set terminals to auto-batch so it never gets missed.

Do I need to change my business bank account?

No. Deposits continue to land in the same account. We just need a voided check or bank letter to route funding.

What do you need from me to open an account?

For the free review, just one recent processing statement. For a full application: basic business and ownership information, a voided check or bank letter, and a government-issued ID for the signer. Most owners complete it in under fifteen minutes.

Security & support

Do you help with chargebacks?

Yes. When a dispute comes in we help you gather the right evidence — signed receipts, delivery confirmation, written terms, communication history — and submit the representment inside the deadline. We also work on prevention: clear billing descriptors, proper authorization capture and terminal settings that reduce risky transactions.

What is the PCI fee on my statement?

PCI fees cover the card industry's data security program. Many merchants are also billed a monthly non-compliance penalty simply because nobody ever completed the annual questionnaire. We walk you through that questionnaire so you stay compliant and stop paying avoidable penalties.

Who do I call when something breaks?

Us — at (614) 419-1601. You get direct phone and text access to the person who set up your account, plus the processor's 24/7 technical line for after-hours emergencies. No ticket queues.

How is my information handled?

Statements and application documents are used only to prepare your analysis and account, and are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. We ask you not to email full card numbers — your statement summary and fee pages are all we need.

What areas do you serve?

We work with businesses across the United States. Statement reviews, onboarding and support are handled by phone, text and email, and on-site visits can be arranged by appointment.
