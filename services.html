Service 01

Credit card processing

Full card acceptance for in-store, mobile and online sales — priced on interchange-plus so you can always see the true cost and the markup separately.

We place accounts with established processors and set pricing that matches how your business actually takes payments: swipe-heavy retail, keyed B2B invoices, high-ticket services or low-ticket volume. Then we document the pricing in writing so there is no ambiguity six months later.

  • Visa · Mastercard · Discover · Amex
  • EMV chip & tap-to-pay
  • Apple Pay & Google Pay
  • PIN debit
  • Level II/III B2B data
  • Next-day funding available

Good fit if you

  • Are on flat-rate or tiered pricing and have never seen an interchange breakdown
  • Have had "rate adjustments" appear without explanation
  • Accept corporate or purchasing cards and want lower B2B interchange

Service 02

Zero-fee & cash discount programs

Keep more of every sale by offering a cash price and a card price — structured the way the card brands require.

Done properly, a cash discount or dual-pricing program can bring your net cost of accepting cards down to almost nothing. Done sloppily, it creates angry customers and compliance problems. We walk you through the rules, handle the terminal programming and signage, and stay available for the first few weeks while your team gets comfortable.

Not every business is a fit — some customer bases simply will not take to it. During your free review we will tell you honestly whether traditional low-rate pricing is the better play.

Service 03

Free statement audits & rate reviews

The service that starts every relationship: a line-by-line audit of what you pay to accept cards.

Send one recent statement and we return a written summary you can actually read — your effective rate, the processor's markup, every recurring fee, and any transactions that downgraded to more expensive interchange categories. You will know precisely where your money is going, whether or not you ever become a client.

Service 04

POS systems & terminals

Hardware chosen for your workflow, configured before it ships, and supported by a person who knows your account.

We build your menu or item catalog, set up modifiers, tip prompts, taxes and user logins, then train your staff so day one is not a scramble. Need inventory counts, table maps, online ordering or appointment booking? We will scope the system around those requirements instead of selling you a box.

We also review any existing lease before you sign anything new — equipment leases are one of the most expensive traps in this industry.

Service 06

Chargeback, PCI & account support

The part most sales reps disappear on. We stay.

When a dispute lands, timing and documentation decide the outcome. We help you assemble receipts, signatures, delivery proof and terms so your response actually stands a chance — and we work on prevention: clear billing descriptors, signed authorizations, and terminal settings that reduce risky transactions.

On the compliance side, we walk you through the annual PCI questionnaire step by step so you stay compliant and stop paying non-compliance fees.
