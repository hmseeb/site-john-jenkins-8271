Our story

Built around one honest conversation

Every relationship here starts the same way: you send a statement, we explain it, and you decide what to do with that information.

The merchant services industry has a reputation problem, and it earned it. Rates quoted as "1.9%" that turn out to be one of three tiers. Multi-year contracts with four-figure cancellation penalties. Equipment leases signed on a tablet in the middle of a lunch rush. Fees that appear quietly in month seven, when nobody is reading the statement anymore.

We built Pro Merchant Savings to work the other direction. We are independent, so we are not locked into selling one platform. We explain pricing in the same terms you would use with your accountant: what came in, what got taken out, and why. And we put the pricing in writing so you can hold us to it.

Most owners we talk to are not looking for the absolute cheapest rate on earth. They want to know they are not being taken advantage of, they want the terminal to work on a Saturday, and they want a phone number that reaches a person who knows their account. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.

How we get paid

Full transparency, because the industry rarely offers it:

  • Your statement analysis is free and carries no obligation.
  • If you become a client, we are compensated by the processor out of the agreed markup — the same markup shown to you in writing.
  • We never charge you a consulting fee to review a statement.
  • Because we are paid over time, keeping your account healthy is how we stay in business.

Our promises

Five things we will always do

Simple commitments that make the rest of the relationship easy.

Tell you the truth

If your current pricing is strong, we will say so and hand you the analysis anyway. A quick honest "no" is worth more than a bad switch.

Put pricing in writing

Your markup, monthly fees and terms are documented before you sign anything. No verbal promises that evaporate later.

Answer the phone

You reach the same number and the same people. Terminal down on a Friday night is not a support-ticket situation.

Re-audit every year

Rates drift and interchange schedules change. We check your statement annually so increases never go unnoticed.

Keep terms month-to-month

No multi-year contracts, no early termination fees. We would rather earn the account every month.
