FAQ

Questions owners ask us first

Straight answers before you ever share a statement.

Is the statement analysis really free?

Yes. There is no fee, no obligation and no commitment. Send one recent processing statement and you get back a written breakdown of your effective rate and every fee on the account. If your current pricing is already competitive, we will tell you and you keep the analysis.

How much can I actually save?

It depends entirely on your current pricing model, card mix and average ticket. Businesses on tiered or flat-rate pricing usually have the most room to improve, while accounts already on tight interchange-plus pricing may have little. That is exactly what the free review tells you — real numbers from your own statement instead of a guess.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. We place accounts on month-to-month terms with no early termination fee. If the service or the pricing ever stops making sense, you are free to leave.

What is a zero-fee or cash discount program?

It is a compliant dual-pricing structure: customers paying with cash receive a discounted price, while the card price covers the cost of acceptance. Set up correctly — with the right signage, receipt language and terminal programming — it can reduce your net processing cost to nearly zero. We handle the compliance details and the customer-facing materials for you.

Will I have to change my bank or my POS system?

Your deposits keep going to the same business bank account. Many popular POS systems can simply be re-pointed to new processing, and when hardware does need to change we program it, help with the menu or item build, and train your staff before you go live.

How long does it take to get set up?

Most applications are approved within one to two business days. Equipment typically ships right after approval, so a straightforward switch is usually running within about a week — and we stay on the phone with you through the first batch and first deposit.
